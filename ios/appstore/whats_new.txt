• Sync server on the phone: collections over 300 MB sync normally. Before, the server asked for a full sync on every sync and then refused the upload with "collection exceeds size limit" (issue #28). The limit is now 3 GB.
• Android: no lag with accessibility services that aren't screen readers, such as password managers and the phone maker's own services. Qt's accessibility support now turns on only for TalkBack and other screen readers (issue #22).
• iPhone, iPad: the app declares the location purpose string App Store Connect asks for.
• Android 11 and older: Kioku no longer closes right after logging in to AnkiWeb, or when a sync ends at once. On a busy or slow phone the sync's notification was taken away before Android had shown it, and Android then closed the app.
• iPhone, iPad: fixed three crashes from TestFlight reports: after a tap on a menu item or button while a text field had the focus, when a dialog closed while scrolling with a trackpad or mouse, and when an add-on opened the list of a combo box that wasn't on screen.
