• iOS, Android: gamepads and game controllers (Bluetooth or USB: Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, 8BitDo…) study and move around Kioku. A shows the answer, then answers Good; B, X and Y answer Again, Hard and Easy; the d-pad moves through the deck list, dialogs and menus. Set each action's buttons by pressing them, per card side, as in AnkiDroid, or turn it off, in Preferences › Advanced › Gamepad › Controls… (Gamepads and Controllers, issue #18).
• iOS: the editor's formatting buttons work on selected text: bold, italic, underline, superscript, subscript, text colour, highlight and remove formatting. MathJax and cloze keep the selected text (issue #17).
• iOS: add-ons can record from the microphone, for example with AVAudioRecorder through rubicon-objc. iOS asks for access the first time; before, it closed Kioku (issue #19).
• iOS: a field keeps its caret when it gets the focus back without a tap (Tab with a keyboard, back from a dialog); the caret went to the end.
• Self-hosted server (Docker): FFmpeg is in the image, so add-ons that use ffmpeg, ffprobe or ffplay work without installing it (issue #15).
• Self-hosted server (Docker): INSTALL_PACKAGES installs Debian packages that add-ons need when the container starts, such as INSTALL_PACKAGES: "imagemagick sox". A restart finds them installed and downloads nothing (issue #15).
• Phones with a narrow screen (such as 720 px wide ones) or a larger interface size: the main screen fits the screen. The top bar, the deck list and the buttons under it were shifted right and cut off (issue #14).
• Android phones with an older system WebView (before Chrome 105, such as Android 11's built-in one without updates): the deck list fits the screen again; its Learn and Due columns ran off the right side.
• iOS, Android: add-ons with a hidden page, such as Contanki, no longer leave a white box over the top left of the main window.
• Phones: the top bar's buttons wrap to a second row when their labels are too long for the screen (Russian, German). They ran off both sides.
• iOS: no crash after shaking the device and choosing Undo. Shake to undo and the three-finger undo gestures are off; use Anki's Undo (Edit menu or Cmd-Z).
• iPad: no crash on a touch after using an Apple Pencil.
• iPad (iPadOS 26): the iPad's menu bar (swipe down from the top of the screen) has Anki's menus, with their ⌘ shortcuts, instead of empty File, Edit, Format, View, Window and Help menus. Browse's menus are there while Browse is open. Anki's own menu row stays; with the iPad's menu bar always shown, turn it off with View › Show Menu Row. Anki's View › Full Screen is called Hide Toolbars on the iPad, next to the iPad's own Window › Full Screen.
• iOS, Android: kioku:// links let other apps and Shortcuts add notes, sync and search, with AnkiMobile's actions and parameters (kioku:// links).
